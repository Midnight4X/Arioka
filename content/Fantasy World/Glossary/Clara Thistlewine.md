**Story:**

- [[Clara Thistlewine]] was a [[Human]] adventurer who served as a [[Classes|Cleric]] for their party. Her party was tasked to take down a B rank dungeon in the [[Cursedlands]]. This dungeon proved to be more difficult than initially anticipated and resulted in most of the party getting beat up with [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] barely able to keep up with healing them. Eventually the group leader decided it wasn't worth it and called a retreat. The party ran through the dungeon to try and escape but the monsters were closing in on them fast and the group leader made the hard choice to sacrifice [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] and pushed her into the monsters so the rest of the party could escape. [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] was ripped apart and killed in that dungeon but because of that her party managed to escape and survive. 

- The Dungeon Master who was a Necromancer stitched [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara's]] torn body back together and revived her. He made her an offer, [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] could leave but in exchange she must bring 20 dead [[Human|humans]] to him. [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] thought doing something like that would be impossible because she can't fight but she had a plan. She would wait for [[Human|humans]] to enter the dungeon and die and bring their bodies to the Dungeon Master first. This method was quite effective and she managed to get the 20 [[Human|humans]] bodies as required to the Dungeon Master. The Dungeon Master not knowing how she managed to get so many was impressed and said she could leave if she wanted but asked her to stay in the dungeon with him. [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] politely declined and said they wished to be free, the Dungeon Master was saddened but agreed that she could go.

- When [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] left she wondered around the [[Cursedlands]] and the other places surrounding it. Eventually she found herself travelling great distances and exploring. She would also visit various lower level dungeons and see if she could find any easy loot. It was in one of these dungeons that she met [[Akari Bloodfallen]] and her party. She expected these people to try and fight her but surprisingly they didn't. [[Akari Bloodfallen|Iris]] was completely unbothered by the fact she was a [[Zombiefolk|zombie]] and asked if she was alright. [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] explained what her party did to her and how she ended up the way she was. [[Akari Bloodfallen|Iris]] then offered [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] a place in her party saying that they were looking for a healer/[[Classes|cleric]] and that now that she was a [[Zombiefolk|zombie]] other people would probably try and hurt her. [[Akari Bloodfallen|Iris]] said that she would protect her and that [[Monsterkin]] need to stick together. [[Clara Thistlewine|Clara]] not really having anywhere else to go decided she would go with [[Akari Bloodfallen|Iris]] and her party to serve as their [[Classes|Cleric]].
