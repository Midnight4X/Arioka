The [[Sol Festival]] is an event that takes place every 2.5 months where the sun will lose its colour and dim before flashing brightly into its new colour that is completely random. The colour that the sun changes into opens a dungeon within one of the [[Forsaken Lands]]. The colours the sun can be are Red, Purple, Green, Blue, Yellow, and White. Not all colours are guaranteed to appear in a year as each colour can appear up to twice in a year. This means some dungeons can be open twice in a year while others wont open at all.






Red - [[Infernum]] - [[Aeloria]]
Purple - [[Veil]] - [[Lustria]]
Green - [[Everwaste]] - [[Vardheim]]
Blue - [[Deadfrost]] - [[Uria]]
Yellow - [[Cursedlands]] - [[Yurei]]
White - Peace
