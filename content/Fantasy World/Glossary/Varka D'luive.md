[[Varka D'luive]] is a small nation of [[Vampire|Vampires]] located in the center of the continent of [[Aeloria]]. They used to be one of the strongest powers on the continent and controlled around 65% of the North but gradually they started losing control and lost war after war as other nations rallied and grew stronger due to the [[Vampire Culling]]. Thanks to the [[Vavakin League]], [[Varka D'luive]] is a shell of its former self and now relies on the [[Vavakin League]] for trade. There are talks about [[Varka D'luive]] joining the [[Vavakin League]] for many years but the talks never end up materializing due to some nations in the league being against it.

[[Varka D'luive]] has a rivalry and grudge towards another [[Vampire]] nation on the continent known as [[Varnisse]] who are located on the West of [[Aeloria]]. They used to be the same nation but broke apart due to conflicting opinions on how to rule the continent and started fighting wars against each other constantly when they were controlling most of the continent. [[Varka D'luive]] was in control of the North while [[Varnisse]] was in control of the South. Even during the [[Vampire Culling]] they still fought each other which made the [[Vampire Culling]] even more effective. Now because of their nations drastic shrinkage they no longer border each other but still hold deep hatred for each other.
