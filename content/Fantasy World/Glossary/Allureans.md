[Lore:]

- The [[Allureans]] are an insanely powerful species who are very persuasive and charming with their words. They are the only [[Vampire]] variant capable of making thralls. 

- It is impossible to be born as an [[Allureans|Allurean]] as the only way an [[Allureans|Allurean]] is created is by drinking the blood of the [[Allureans|Allurean Queen]] with her consent. If the Queen is dead the only other way of becoming an [[Allureans|Allurean]] is by winning the [[Allureans|Allurean]] Throne Tournament and then drinking the stored blood of old [[Allureans|Allurean Queens]]. If a [[Vampire]] drinks [[Allureans|Allurean]] blood without getting permission from the Queen or winning the tournament then the blood will melt that [[Vampire|Vampires]] body from the inside out.

- The [[Allureans|Allurean Queen]] will give her blood to a [[Vampire]] she deems worthy of being an heir. Once an heir has been chosen they will become Queen once the current one dies. Multiple heirs can be chosen and when the Queen dies they will fight to see who takes the throne. If an heir loses to another but survives they will go back to whatever [[Vampire]] variant they were prior to being chosen. It is generally frowned upon to accept more than one heir however so it is rarely ever done.
