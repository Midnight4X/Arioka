[Lore:]

- The [[Vavakin League]] was originally formed as a defensive alliance to protect against a [[Vampire]] nation known as [[Varka D'luive]] that is also located in the north. However that was no longer necessary since the war on [[Vampire|Vampires]] around the world ended so the alliance evolved to be solely a diplomatic and trade alliance though the [[Kingdom of Sulmanite]] still acts as a protectorate of the alliance to protect northern interest from the carnage in the south. They now do trade with [[Varka D'luive]] as the hostilities over many many years have all but seized and now they are valued business partners. There are also talks for many years of eventually letting them join the League but pushback from certain nations have constantly prevented it.
