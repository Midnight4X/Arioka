[Lore:]

- [[High Elves]] are the rarest of all the [[Elves|Elven]] races. Despite their low numbers, they hold immense power and prestige due to their unmatched mastery of magic and extremely long lifespans. [[High Elves]] are considered the ruling class within [[Elves|Elven]] society, and nearly all positions of major political power are controlled by them and the [[Fairy|Fairies]].

- [[High Elves]] see themselves as stewards of [[Elves|Elvenkind]], believing their mastery of magic and elevated intelligence make them the only ideal people to rule the rest of the [[Elves]]. They are an extremely elitist people and are extremely Xenophobic to anyone who is not an [[Elyrian]]. This leads to most [[Elves|Elven]] Kingdoms being very isolationist to other nations outside of [[Elyrian]] lands.

- [[High Elves]] have very strong ties with [[Fairy|Fairies]] and will often have shared governments and shared societies. It is common practice for a [[High Elves|High Elf]] to be paired up with a [[Fairy]] at childhood who then grow up together as partners. Other [[Elves|Elven]] races can be paired up with with a [[Fairy]] but it is harder. Either they need to earn it by achieving great feats or if a [[Fairy]] specifically chooses them and binds themselves which isn't unheard of but is a very rare occurrence.
