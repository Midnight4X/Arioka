Gender: Female

[[Lady Seraphine]] is one of the 3 daughters of [[Equilon]]. She is [[Gods|Goddess]] of Dark and with the help of her sister [[Lady Aurelia]] they create life itself together. She is responsible for the Night and if she is in a bad mood the nights will last longer. She is also the [[Gods|Goddess]] who manages the [[Seven Apostates]] and makes sure they stay in check. She is reserved and quiet in nature opting to watch her fellow [[Gods]] from a distance and only engaging when necessary or if approached first. She's particularly fond of the species living in the [[Underworld]] and those who roam and enjoy the night.
