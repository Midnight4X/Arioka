Editor Notes:

Rising through the ranks isn't only about raw power but also your experience and accomplishments. Once you've gained enough experience and accomplishments to go up a rank you must take a test to see if you have the strength for the next rank.


Or

Complete a certain amount of dungeons of your rank to qualify to take a test for the next rank. If you fail the test you must compete the amount of dungeons again. The test exists to prevent people getting carried through ranks.
