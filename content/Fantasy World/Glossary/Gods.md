[Lore:]

- Balance controls time and fate
- dark and light create life
- Silence and Solitude are siblings




Balance Name = Equilon or Solnyx
Dark Name = Lady Erelith or Seraphine
Light Name = Lady Aurelia
Dead Sins = The Seven Apostates
Holy Sins = The Seven Venerators 



The idea behind the hierarchy is that for example the holy church could worship the Goddess of Light and there could be branches of that church that worship individual Venerators but they all still are under the faith of the Light Goddess.

Athiesm is pretty much entirely none existent in Arioka so the closest you can really get is someone who is under the Faith of the God of Balance who wouldn't worship light or dark.
