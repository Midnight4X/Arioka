**Lore:**

- [[Pippa Greenkin]] is a [[Goblina]] who has spent much of her life in captivity, serving as a slave. She managed to escape captivity when [[Akari Bloodfallen]] attacked a slaver convoy she was being transported in. It was then that [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari]] offered for her to join their party as [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari's]] personal Squire. [[Pippa Greenkin|Pippa]] saw [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari]] as someone she owed her life to and decided she would accept and learn how to live outside of captivity with the help of [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari]].

- [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari's]] party members will sometimes jokingly refer to [[Pippa Greenkin]] as [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari's Teddy Bear]].
