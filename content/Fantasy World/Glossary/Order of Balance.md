#religion

The [[Order of Balance]] is a [[Religion]] dedicated to the [[Gods|God]] [[Equilon]] and all the [[Gods]] they control. It is the [[Religion]] that people pick when they aren't dedicated to any one faith as it simply covers the belief that the [[Gods]] themselves exist and as a result it is one of the lowest denounced [[Religion|Religions]] in [[Arioka]].

There is a much smaller group in the [[Order of Balance]] who worship specifically [[Equilon]] and not all other [[Gods]]. They are quite fanatical and dangerous and because of their rare dedication to [[Equilon]] himself [[Equilon]] will sometimes use them in his pursuit of balance.
