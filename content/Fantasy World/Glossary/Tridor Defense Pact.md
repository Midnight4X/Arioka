#alliance

The [[Tridor Defense Pact]] is an defensive alliance formed by 3 countries on the east of [[Lustria]].

[Countries:]

- [[Kingdom of Highmark]]
- [[Dawnriver Kingdom]]
- [[Kingdom of Bryndor]]

[Affiliates:]

- None

[Lore:]

- The [[Kingdom of Highmark]] and [[Dawnriver Kingdom]] are very similar culturally and allow non tolled travel between the 2 countries. Because of this they get along quite well. The [[Kingdom of Bryndor]] however does not get along with the other 2 nations and there is often friction between them. However due to how volatile East [[Lustria]] is they are left with no choice but to remain in a defensive alliance with the [[Kingdom of Highmark]] and [[Dawnriver Kingdom]] or they would risk being wiped out by another nation or alliance.
