#realm 

The [[Empyrean]] is the realm of the [[Gods]] that is completely inaccessible by all beings that are not [[Gods]] or [[Exalted Champions]] except under special circumstances. 

Mortal beings on the verge of death can appear in [[Empyrean]] if one of the [[Gods]] has taken an interest in them and brought them to [[Empyrean]] from the verge of [[Lady Mortella]]'s Gate. This is mostly to make a deal with them to spare their lives in exchange for something to further the select [[Gods|Gods']] interest.
