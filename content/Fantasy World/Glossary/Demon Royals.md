[Lore:]

- Contrary to their name [[Demon Royals]] are not actually related to [[Demon|Demons]]. They gained their name many millennia's ago because they enslaved all the [[Demon]] races and ruled over them. They are actually more closely related to [[Vampire|Vampires]] and it is theorized they originated from [[Vampire|Vampires]] crossbreeding with some other long extinct ancient horned species.

- After ruling over [[Demon|Demons]] for a few centuries the [[Demon Royals]] found it counterproductive and tedious so they eventually left the [[Realm of Races]] and returned to the [[Sky Islands]]. For a long time they would descend down on the [[Realm of Races]] to cause havoc before returning back to the [[Sky Islands]]. However the [[Harpy|Harpies]] kept gaining in strength and numbers and the [[Demon Royals]] got complacent and didn't take them out before they became too big of a threat. Now the [[Harpy|Harpies]] and [[Demon Royals]] are in constant war.

- The [[Demon Royals]] control all of the [[Seven Daedric Islands]]. The outer 6 are for the 6 Demon Royal Factions and the inner island is for the Ruler of the [[Demon Royals]] and acts as the central hub.
