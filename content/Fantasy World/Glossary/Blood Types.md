The blood type you are born with determines what kind of magic you are able to use. It can also have certain effects on the body or others. It is entirely possible to have mixed blood in any combination.

---

**Blood Types:**

[Blessed Blood]

- Blessed Blood enables the use of Light Magic. It is a very rare blood type and those who have it are highly sought after due to the rarity and strength of light magic. This blood type can also act as a poison for species that are weak against light magic.

[Scorched Blood]

- Scorched Blood enables the use of Fire Magic.  People with this blood type have higher body temperatures compared to what their species would normally have.
