[Lore:]

- [[Pixie|Pixies]] are closely related to [[Fairy|Fairies]] but unlike [[Fairy|Fairies]] their society does not have a rigid hierarchy. [[Pixie|Pixies]] normally live in small tribes scattered across the forests they occupy. They spend most of their time tending to their glade and homes, playing games with each other, dancing and singing in circles, visiting other [[Pixie]] groups and also playing pranks on random adventurers or other forest species using illusion magic as they are very mischievous and playful.

- [[Pixie|Pixies]] are the only societal species known that [Anarchy] worked for. This is mostly due to their innate nature as a species that all get along and all have great respect for the forest. They know when a prank is going to far and will stop, They will also honor and respect ancient places, sacred stones and old glades. They also all have a mutual love for story telling and humor. All this combined with a love for freedom yet deep care for one another even for [[Pixie]] tribes that haven't met before lead to a working [Anarchical] society.
