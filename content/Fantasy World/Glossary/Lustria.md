[[Lustria]] is the continent known as the birthplace of [[Vampire|Vampires]]. Over half of the continent is controlled by [[Allurea]] while the other half is controlled by various other nations that occupied the land once [[Allurea]] abandoned it and retreated to the [[Lurea Mountains]] to end the [[Vampire Culling]]. These other nations are both new formed nations and existing nations from other continents. There are often wars over what land there is due to the land being rich in natural resources compared to other continents.
