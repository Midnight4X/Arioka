Hair color is always a darker version of their skin color

2 horns on forehead

Front teeth are human like, canine teeth are elongated and back teeth are pointy

Tongue, tips of horns, tips of fingers, tips of toes and tip of tail are the same color as eyes unless they are a a tribal version in that case only the tongue is the same color

Skin can be pale blue, pale purple, pale red, pale orange, pale yellow

Eye color can be any bright colour except for the bright version of their skin colour. For example they can have pale purple skin but cannot have bright purple eyes.


Tribals wear mostly pelt and leather outfits with a lot of skin showing
Regulars wear much more normal clothing that you would see humans wear.

Tribals pupils' are a black "X" while regulars pupils' are a black plus
