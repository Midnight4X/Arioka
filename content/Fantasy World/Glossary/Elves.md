[Lore:]

- [[Elves]] have 4 races that have innate differences than just looks. The different races are: [[Wood Elves]], [[Dark Elves]], [[High Elves]], [[Low Elves]]. The races act and work differently in their respective societies as well as when together. The population of each elf varies greatly with the percentage being:  68% [[Low Elves]], 20% [[Wood Elves]], 12% [[Dark Elves]]. The [[High Elves]] are significantly lower in numbers.

- [[Elves]] are not as physically strong as [[Human|Humans]] so they tend to avoid melee combat, opting for ranged combat with either magic, archery or both. Archery is generally preferred by [[Low Elves]] and [[Dark Elves]] while magic is preferred by [[High Elves]]. However [[Wood Elves]] generally use both methods of combat equally.
