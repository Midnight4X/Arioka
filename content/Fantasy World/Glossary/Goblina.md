[Lore:]

- [[Goblina]] are closely related to their Monster counterparts [Goblins] however they are not influenced by the [Goblins] innate desire for Gold, Bloodthirst, Women and Violence. Instead they are a very peaceful people in comparison. They mostly trade goods from their villages and do menial tasks/jobs for other species. [[Goblina|Goblina's]] particularly exceed in job trades such as Alchemy, Tailorship or Woodworking. 

- [[Goblina|Goblinas]] used to have a slavery problem due to their naturally strong grip strength and small size but ever since the [[West Uria Alliance]] in [[Uria]] banned the sale of Slaves within the [[West Uria Alliance]] the problem isn't as bad as it used to be especially once [[Goblina|Goblinas]] started migrating to the Nations within the [[West Uria Alliance]].

- There are 3 types of [[Goblina|Goblinas]]; [[Forest Goblina|Forest Goblinas]], [[Desert Goblina|Desert Goblinas]], and [[Mountain Goblina|Mountain Goblinas]]. The different types of [[Goblina|Goblinas]] are best suited for the environment in their names however that does not mean they cannot be found in their non native habitat.

- The [[Goblina]] are very commonly opposed to the [[Diligence]] God because of their species' problem with being used as slaves and forced to work hard against their will. This even includes non enslaved [[Goblina]] who still work hard.
