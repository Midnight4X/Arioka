[Lore:]

- [[Dark Elves]] are the least populous of the 3 most populous [[Elves|Elven]] species. They are mostly mixed in with [[Low Elves]] in cities/towns or found in other none [[Elves|Elven]] societies. Many [[Dark Elves]] are highly independent, having long been discriminated against in [[Elves|Elven]] society. Due to [[Dark Elves]] natural skillsets they are fairly involved in the criminal underground taking on jobs as Rogues or Dark Mages to make a living outside of [[Elves|Elven]] territory. They have learnt to survive in hostile environments, becoming adept at manipulation, secrecy, and deception. This makes them exceptional spies, assassins, and information brokers although not all [[Dark Elves]] choose such paths. Discrimination against [[Dark Elves]] has died down significantly over the years but because of the past many [[Dark Elves]] are inside of criminal families as those scars are yet to fully heal.
