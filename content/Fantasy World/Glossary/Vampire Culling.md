#event

An event where most of the species around the world united against the [[Vampire|Vampires]] and wiped most of them out and reduced [[Vampire|Vampires]] to a shell of their former selves. The [[Vampire Culling]] was stopped after [[Allurea]] made a treaty called The [[Blood Cession]] to order all [[Vampire]] nations stop their conquests and to surrender part of the [[Lustria]] continent.
