- [[Dystan-iluminos]] is a nomadic [[Vampire]] clan whose entire lineage was cursed by the [[Allurea|Overlord of Vampires]] after they tried to steal some [[Allureans|Allurean]] blood. They have no land to their name and aren't recognized by any other [[Vampire]] factions as an official noble family due to their transgressions. They live inside makeshift cabins inside of which ever forest they deem safe to settle in for awhile. They hide away from other [[Vampire|Vampires]] because if they are discovered to be alive the [[Allurea|Overlord of Vampires]] may come and destroy them all for finding a way to survive.

- The curse that was placed upon them was supposed to eventually kill them off by slowly contaminating their blood until their bodies could no longer function and eventually perish. This curse worked at first but as more and more of the [[Dystan-iluminos]] clan started dying they eventually found a solution to survive. If they forcibly remove the contaminated blood out of their bodies and replace it with a healthy [[Human|humans']] blood then it resets the contamination cycle allowing them to continue living. In order to not be weakened or perish they have to repeat this 3 times a year on average to make sure their blood is always healthy enough to function. Depending on the blood that they take they can actually use elemental magic they weren't able to prior so if they see [[Human|humans]] who have desirable elemental magic they will generally target that [[Human|human]]. This however does not apply to Blessed Blood. Due to [[Vampire|Vampires]] having an inherent weakness to light magic if they swap their blood with someone who possesses Blessed Blood it will kill them almost instantly.

- The [[Dystan-iluminos]] spend most of their time hunting for food and researching ways to remove this curse placed upon their Lineage. They are extremely hostile to any outsiders because discovery is so dangerous to their survival. They will kill anyone and anything they think saw them even if they didn't. The one exception is [[Human|humans]] when it is time to swap blood. They will either trick [[Human|humans]] into returning home with them or ambush them and forcibly restrain them to then transfer their blood which kills the [[Human|humans]] in the process anyway.
