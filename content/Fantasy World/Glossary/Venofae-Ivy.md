[Lore:]

- [[Venofae-Ivy]] are a variant of [[Alraune]] that can be found in the [[Cursedlands]], Monster Forests and on rare occasions in Dungeons. They ambush and trap their prey using tentacles they have burrowed into the ground. Once they have trapped their prey they will use their tentacles to drag them towards their pod and then begin pumping a very deadly liquid into the the prey's mouth. This liquid melts the body from the inside out including the bones and is immensely painful.

- The [[Venofae-Ivy]] are hyper aggressive and cannot be reasoned with. They are also much stronger physically than their counterpart the [[Sapsuri]] leading to a much higher fatality rate in comparison.
