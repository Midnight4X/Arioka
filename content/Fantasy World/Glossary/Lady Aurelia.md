#deity 

Gender: Female

[[Lady Aurelia]] is one of the 3 daughters of [[Equilon]]. She is the [[Gods|Goddess]] of Light and with the help of her sister [[Lady Seraphine]] they create life itself together. She is responsible for the Day and if she is in a bad mood the temperature of where the sun is shining will increase. She is also the [[Gods|Goddess]] who manages the [[Seven Venerators]] and makes sure they stay in check. She is bright, cheerful and bubbly in nature and is always speaking with whoever she can. While she acts like she doesn't favor any particular species she is actually quite fond of the [[Demihuman|Demihumans]] and [[Human|Humans]].
