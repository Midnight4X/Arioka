#location 
The [[Forsaken Lands]] consists of 5 areas of land spread across the 5 continents. When the sun changes colour every 2.5 months during the [[Sol Festival]] a gate will open to a very large dungeon in the very center of these lands. Which area of land the gate appears in is completely random and is determined by what colour the sun is. These lands are very huge and very treacherous to navigate and survive. Very dangerous monsters will flow out of these gate and occupy whichever [[Forsaken Lands|Forsaken Land]] it is inside of making getting to the dungeon a challenge and all existing monsters in the lands gain strength as well. This also drives monsters to leave the [[Forsaken Lands]] wreaking havoc on anything in their path. Countries will mobilize entire armies to go into the [[Forsaken Lands]] to try neutralize the dungeon. When there is not a dungeon, monsters still very much roam their lands but they are not as strong and won't leave their lands and aren't as plentiful. This leads to them being prime areas for country's militaries to train against monsters as well as navigation through the lands. This also leads to them being prime areas to collect monster materials outside of regular dungeons and normal monster lands.

- [[Uria]] - [[Deadfrost]] - Blue
- [[Aeloria]] - [[Infernum]] - Red
- [[Vardheim]] - [[Everwaste]] - Green
- [[Yurei]] - [[Cursedlands]] - Yellow
- [[Lustria]] - [[Veil]] - Purple
