**Backstory**:

- [[Akari Bloodfallen]] is a [[Silvers|Silver Vampire]] who was born into the [[Bloodfallen|Bloodfallen Clan]]. She was born with mismatched eyes which interested her family greatly as a [[Vampire]] having an eye colour other than red or orange was unheard of let alone 2 differently coloured eyes. As she grew up it became apparent that she possessed immense intelligence for learning and immense strength. She studied many topics and languages as well as trained swordsmanship. She loved how rapiers felt and that became her main weapon. She practiced how to use her magic with her rapiers and became an incredibly powerful being though no rapier could withstand the magic for too long and they would break at the tip a lot due to the way she channeled magic.. Her family decided to have the best [[Vampire]] blacksmith create a rapier specifically for her and it was an incredibly beautiful yet strong piece of weaponry that was actually capable of channeling magic the way [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari]] wanted. They also made a full face mask for her, engraved with various patterns and symbols that represented the history of the [[Bloodfallen|Bloodfallen Clan]]. It was made so she could go out into the world and explore without the complications that come with being a [[Vampire]]. She was also training to become the heir to the [[Allureans|Allurean]] throne as she was well liked by the [[Allureans|Allurean Queen]]. However life eventually took a turn for the worst. A freak accident still unknown to [[Akari Bloodfallen|Akari]] caused her to be teleported to an entirely different continent and was stripped of all her memories. She instinctively knew how to use her magic and how to fight as well as maintained her knowledge of languages and other things yet she simply cannot remember how she learnt these things or who she is. Now she is all alone stranded on the continent of [[Uria]] and the only thing she has from her past is the [[Bloodfallen]] mask and her name. The rapier that she once cherished remained in the [[Bloodfallen|Bloodfallen Clan]] Estate in a safe place while her family tries to find her.

**Facts:**

- [[Akari Bloodfallen]] is the name given to her by her [[Vampire]] family. [[Akari Bloodfallen|Ayame Tatsuki]] is the name given to her by her adoptive [[Harpy]] family. [[Akari Bloodfallen|Iris Blackheart]] is her adventuring name that is registered at the guild.

- [[Akari Bloodfallen]] can regain some memories from consuming blood over a long enough period of time. However they are very far and few between. Finding her family might be the only way to regain 95% of her memory.

- [[Akari Bloodfallen]]'s right eye is known as "The Eye of Ether". It is Grey in colour and contains powerful magic properties. It has various abilities that can be used and when used the eye changes to the corresponding colour. When using the abilities depending on how much power you are using determines how much of the iris gets coloured. For example if you use 20% of the blue power then 20% of the iris will be blue scattered around starting from the outer iris while the other 80% is grey. 2 Abilities can also be used at the same time which causes the Iris to be 2 different colours other than grey but in this instance the colour is not scattered around and instead starts from the left and right sides of the outer iris respectively and makes their way to the inner iris depending on how much power is being used.
