A security and trade alliance based in the continent of [[Lustria]] and underworld of the [[Abyss]].

[Countries:]

- [[Allurea]]
- [[Nox Fortuna]]

[Affiliates:]

- None

[Lore:]

- The [[Lurean Compact]] was formed to help guarantee the security of the [[Lustria]] continent. It was formed long ago before the [[Vampire Culling]] and when it was originally created [[Nox Fortuna]] was much weaker but had the best relationship with [[Allurea]] vs the other [[Abyss]] factions so [[Allurea]] decided to help strengthen them by providing resources, training and manpower. Eventually after many many years, [[Nox Fortuna]] controlled a majority of the [[Abyss]] and once that had happened [[Allurea]] stopped providing resources, training and manpower and instead transitioned their alliance to one of mutual security and trade now that [[Nox Fortuna]] was strong enough. Because of their pleasant history together they are still very close allies and [[Nox Fortuna]] hold great respect for [[Allurea]] especially for all the help they provided as well as the fact they are still a much stronger nation than [[Nox Fortuna]].

- [[Nox Fortuna]] was critical to the defense of [[Lustria]] and [[Allurea]] during the [[Vampire Culling]]. Because of their strength and knowledge of the underground the rest of the world's nations were unable to attack [[Allurea]] through the underground which allowed [[Allurea]] to only focus on the surface which was the main reason they never lost any land over battle.
