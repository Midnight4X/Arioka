#deity

Gender: Male

[[Equilon]] is the most powerful [[Gods|God]] in the [[Empyrean]] and was the one who brought order and balance to all the [[Gods]] through his own power and the birth of his 3 daughters who manage all the other [[Gods]]. He controls time and fate in [[Arioka]] and is responsible for ensuring the world stays at a balance. It is suspected that it was [[Equilon]]'s influence that gave many species the strength to carry out the [[Vampire Culling]] and it is also suspected that the same was done to the [[Demon Royals]] when they got too powerful for the world.  It is still a mystery to even his own daughters how he makes his decisions to make sure the world is balanced or what he even considers to be balanced. [[Equilon]] also helped [[Wrath]] create the [[Forsaken Lands]] to use as a tool for his concept of "balance".
