[Lore:]

- [[Fairy|Fairies]] are closely related to [[Pixie|Pixies]] but unlike [[Pixie|Pixies]] they have a clear and strong hierarchy. Their society is very similar to the [[Elves]] in terms of the hierarchy and royalty.  They either live in isolation, small villages made into the trees or other surroundings, and "big" cities also made into the trees or other surroundings. For the [[Fairy|Fairies]] who live in isolation they are either on assignments or were banished/exiled from their society.

- [[Fairy|Fairies]] have very strong ties with [[Elves]] and will often have shared governments and shared societies. It is common practice for a [[High Elves|High Elf]] to be paired up with a [[Fairy]] at childhood who then grow up together as partners. Other [[Elves|Elven]] races can be paired up with with a [[Fairy]] but it is harder. Either they need to earn it by achieving great feats or if a [[Fairy]] specifically chooses them and binds themselves which isn't unheard of but is a very rare occurrence.
