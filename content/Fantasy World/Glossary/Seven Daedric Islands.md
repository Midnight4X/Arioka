- The [[Seven Daedric Islands]] are ruled by the [[Demon Royals]] and is the place they originated from. Each major outer island is each home to a major faction within the [[Demon Royals]] and is named after them. Meanwhile the middle island is shared between all of them and is where the ruler of the [[Demon Royals]] rules from. It acts as the central hub for all of the other factions and people of the [[Demon Royals]].

- The [[Seven Daedric Islands]] are also the center point of the [[Sky Islands]] and what all the other islands rotate around. At one point in history the [[Seven Daedric Islands]] was just one gigantic circular island before they were split apart in an unknown cataclysmic event called the [[Great Cataclysm]] which caused the [[Sky Islands]] and [[Seven Daedric Islands]] to appear in the sky and be revealed to the world. It was also this event that made the [[Demon Royals]] descend down to the [[Realm of Races]] to enslave the [[Demon]] race and cause havoc upon the world.

- Out of all of the [[Demon Royals|Demon Royal]] territory the [[Seven Daedric Islands]] is the absolute strongest. [[Harpy|Harpies]] have attempted attacks on the [[Seven Daedric Islands]] but due to the large distance between the [[Seven Daedric Islands]] and the rest of the [[Sky Islands]] as well as how heavily fortified they are and the sheer amount of [[Demon Royals]] inhabiting them the attacks were completely pointless. Because of that the [[Harpy|Harpies]] stopped attacking the [[Seven Daedric Islands]] completely and focused all their attention on the [[Sky Islands]].
