[Lore:]

- The largest and most powerful nation of [[Vampire|Vampires]] on the planet located on the continent of [[Lustria]]. They control and possess over half of the entire continent. They would have controlled the entire continent if not for the [[Vampire Culling]] as in order for the [[Vampire Culling]] to end they had to allow other species to have their own nations on the continent so they surrendered around 40% of the continent from the east side and pulled their borders back to the [[Lurea Mountains]]. Most of the entire [[Vampire]] population is located within their borders as well as most of the [[Vampire]] clans.

- [[Nox Fortuna]] was critical to the defense of [[Lustria]] and [[Allurea]] during the [[Vampire Culling]]. Because of their strength and knowledge of the underground the rest of the world's nations were unable to attack [[Allurea]] through the underground which allowed [[Allurea]] to only focus on the surface which was the main reason they never lost any land over battle.

- In the aftermath of the humiliating [[Vampire Culling]], [[Allurea]] did not wait idly. They spent their time isolating themselves and getting stronger. They went through multiple [[Allureans|Allurean]] Queens and planned to someday break the [[Blood Cession]] and retake the continent of [[Lustria]]. However they never ended up doing that. Over time the mindset changed from attempting world domination to ensuring the safety of what is left of the [[Vampire|Vampires]]. Because of that mindset change they are hoping that someday they are able to negotiate an end to the [[Blood Cession]] so the may retake the continent of [[Lustria]] in its entirety. While that goal would have been impossible originally, the fact that the [[Demon Royals]] appeared and united the world against them just like what had happened to the [[Vampire|Vampires]] during the [[Vampire Culling]] brings hope that they can use it as an opportunity to bring the treaty to an end.

- [[Allurea]] has 4 massive walls built in the [[Lurea Mountain Pass]]. There used to be 2 but after the [[Vampire Culling]] they built an additional 2. 1 at the far east and one at the far west.
