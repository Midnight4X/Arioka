[Lore:]

[[Nox Fortuna]] are the strongest faction of the [[Nox]] species and the strongest underworld faction in the [[Abyss]] located underneath [[Lustria]]. They are close allies with [[Allurea]] due to [[Allurea]] not wanting to deal with maintaining security underground so [[Nox Fortuna]] takes care of that for them and trades stuff from the [[Abyss]] and in return [[Allurea]] provides many amenities and resources from the surface to them.
