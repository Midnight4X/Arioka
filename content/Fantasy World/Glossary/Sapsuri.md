[Lore:]

- [[Sapsuri]] are a variant of [[Alraune]] that can be found in [[Elyrian]] Forests, Monster Forests and on rare occasions inside Dungeons. They seduce their targets and dull their mind with aphrodisiac gas. They get their targets to climb inside their flower pod with them and start draining their lifeforce which is completely painless. Once their lifeforce has been drained they will then absorb the body using the liquid inside the pod and then pick out the bones and throw them away.

- Between the 2 [[Alraune]] variants the [[Sapsuri]] are the only ones that can be reasoned with. It is entirely possible to communicate with a [[Sapsuri]] and avoid a conflict though rare.
