[Lore:]

- [[Wood Elves]] are the [[Elves]] who have the closest relation to nature and the forest. Most are found in settlements away from the main [[Elves|Elven]] cities and towns. While [[Elves]] have a close relationship with [[Fairy|Fairies]], [[Wood Elves]] don't really share that connection. Instead [[Wood Elves]] are much closer in relationship to [[Pixie|Pixies]] however unlike [[Fairy|Fairies]], [[Pixie|Pixies]] will not bind themselves to any [[Elves|Elf]] including [[Wood Elves]] as [[Pixie|Pixies]] value freedom above all else. [[Wood Elves]] are also known to be on good terms and occasionally work with [[Dryads]] and the [[Petalfae]]

- [[Wood Elves]] generally don't have a good relationship with the big [[Elves|Elven]] Kingdoms as they do not agree with how they built, believing that [[Elves]] should live as one with nature rather than destroying nature to build large cities. This has lead to many conflicts in the past and in recent however due to having less numbers the [[Wood Elves]] eventually have to concede.
