[Lore:]

[[Vampire|Vampires]]:

- [[Vampire|Vampires]] have 5 variants; [[Silvers]], [[Crimsons]], [[Allureans]], [[Solarins]] and [[Thralls]]. [[Thralls]] make up 72% of [[Vampire|Vampires]] while [[Silvers]] make up 16%, [[Solarins]] 10% and [[Crimsons]] 2%. [[Crimsons]] are generally much more aggressive than the other [[Vampire]] species and possess greater physical strength in exchange for less magic power. While being born a [[Silvers|Silver]] or [[Solarins|Solarin]] is related to your blood lineage it is actually completely random if a [[Vampire]] is born as a [[Crimsons|Crimson]] and has no relation to bloodlines. [[Allureans]] however cannot be born and are better described as created. 

- [[Vampire|Vampires]] and [[Harpy|Harpies]] hate each other deeply due to years of fighting against each other. They only stopped fighting due to reaching an agreement to halt fighting so the [[Harpy|Harpies]] could focus on the war against the [[Demon Royals]]. However that resentment is still there and when [[Vampire|Vampires]] and [[Harpy|Harpies]] meet it tends to lead to judgmental looks, arguments and fights.

- [[Vampire|Vampires]] while capable of regular magic, use blood magic instead as they have a greater natural affinity for it and is therefore more powerful and comfortable option for them.

- Long ago far before the [[Demon Royals]] ever appeared, [[Vampire|Vampires]] used to rule over the [[Demon]] species and are responsible for the universal [[Demon]] language. Eventually however it seemed that controlling all the [[Demon]] races were problematic and that if [[Vampire|Vampires]] continued to rule over the [[Demon|Demons]] then most of the world would likely unite against them so they distanced themselves and focused on their own kind instead and became quite isolationist in the process. To this day however the [[Demon]] language is still widely used as it is mostly the only language [[Thralls]] speak while [[Vampire]] nobility know both Common and [[Demon]].

- [[Vampire|Vampires]] used to be at war with the world much like how the [[Demon Royals]] are to this day. They used the various [[Demon]] species they ruled over as cannon fodder in their wars and controlled nearly half the world. They also had many [[Monsterkin]] in their ranks. However due to the other species becoming more willing to work together to fight back and the fact that the [[Demon]] species were getting harder and harder to control they started to lose more and more territory. Now, many [[Vampire]] nations are spread across all the continents but are shells of their former selves and might of been wiped out completely if not for the [[Vampire Culling]] ending.

- [[Vampire|Vampires]] as a species are a Matriarchal society. This is mostly due to females being significantly stronger than the males, with males being considered lesser beings. There is only one [[Vampire]] faction led by a male and that is [[Cerulean Skar]]. It is mostly made up of males who deserted their respective clans/factions due to mistreatment or desire for more power.

- [[Vampire|Vampires]] can drink the blood of any species to survive. They can go a week until their next feeding before they get hungry again. The way [[Vampire|Vampires]] taste is very different and refined specifically for blood meaning blood from different species can taste vastly different from another which leads to [[Vampire|Vampires]] having different preferences for which species they enjoy the blood from the most. Normal food however tastes very bland in comparison so extravagant [[Vampire]] parties will generally have food made entirely out of different types of blood.

- [[Vampire|Vampires]] are weakened by the sun. When exposed to the sun it reduces their power and makes them sleepy. Because of this most [[Vampire|Vampires]] opt to sleep during the day and stay awake at night. [[Solarins]] however are actually immune to this and there are some very rare cases of some of the other [[Vampire]] variants being immune to the sun as well and are known as daywalkers. There are also partial daywalkers who instead of getting weakened and drowsy in the sun they just get drowsy.

- The birthplace of [[Vampire|Vampires]] is the continent of [[Lustria]]. To this day the continent has a huge [[Vampire]] presence mainly because of [[Allurea]]. 

- [[Thralls]] are the only [[Vampire]] species that reproduce in the common way. The other [[Vampire]] species reproduce by getting either a [[Human]] baby or [[Thralls|Thrall]] baby and then both partners will feed their blood to the baby while infusing blood magic into the blood. This the causes the baby to completely shift in appearance and rewrites their whole DNA before finally becoming the child of whatever [[Vampire]] species choose them. The transformation process would be incredibly painful if it wasn't for the numbing effect [[Vampire]] blood has. By the end of the transformation the baby [[Vampire]] will have grown small baby fangs, tiny black nails, changed eye colour, and their skin turned a pale white. [[Vampire|Vampires]] used to take babies forcefully and some nations still do however the vast majority instead cultivated a society that babies would be donated to them. Most of the time it is unwanted babies or babies that lost both parents that are donated.
