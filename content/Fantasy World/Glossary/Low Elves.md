[Lore:]

- [[Low Elves]] are the most numerous of all the [[Elves|Elven]] races, making up a vast 68% of the total population. They form the backbone of [[Elves|Elven]] society, working as farmers, artisans, merchants, guards, scribes, and more. It is also [[Low Elves]] who are the most likely to leave to leave their society to migrate to other none [[Elyrian]] countries as tradesmen or adventurers. 

- While [[Low Elves]] are often viewed as commoners within the social hierarchy, they do not resent this status. In fact, many admire the [[High Elves]] and view them with deep reverence, seeing them as paragons of [[Elves|Elvenkind]] whose wisdom and magical prowess set a high standard for all [[Elves]]. The ambition to emulate [[High Elves]] in discipline, education, or magical skill is common among [[Low Elves]], and this aspiration fuels a strong cultural work ethic. Many [[Low Elves]] send their children to academies run or influenced by [[High Elves]], hoping to earn distinction and recognition. It is not uncommon for a [[Low Elves|Low Elf]] to dedicate their life in service to a [[High Elves|High Elf]] noble house, often with great pride rather than resentment. Such roles are considered honorable among the [[Low Elves|Low Elf]] communities.
