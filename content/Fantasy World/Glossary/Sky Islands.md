- The [[Sky Islands]] is a huge mass of over 1000 islands of varying sizes far up in the sky that orbit around the [[Seven Daedric Islands]]. They are located 13750 meters above sea level and travel from East to West at a rate of 0.856m/s or 3.08km/h. They are completely inaccessible for races that are none flying but can be seen from the ground on clear days. 

- The war over the [[Sky Islands]] has lasted over 5000 years and both sides have been locked in a stalemate occasionally taking and losing land respectively. The [[Harpy|Harpies]] are physically and arcanically much weaker than the [[Demon Royals]] but have much much greater numbers. The [[Demon Royals]] have an extremely low birth rate and fertility so they can't outgrow the [[Harpy|Harpies]] which is the reason they have been stuck in a stalemate for so long. The [[Harpy|Harpies]] are also backed by almost every species in the [[Realm of Races]] in terms of training, supplies and equipment. They do this because as long as the [[Harpy|Harpies]] are fighting the [[Demon Royals]] the [[Demon Royals]] cannot risk leaving the [[Sky Islands]].
