#location

The [[Lurea Mountain Pass]] is a defensive set of 4 walls built by [[Allurea]] to prevent invasion past the [[Lurea Mountains]]. It was originally just 2 massive walls but after the [[Vampire Culling]] they built an additional 2 to the Far East and Far West. 

The walls used to be called Lurean Wall 1 and Lurean Wall 2 but during the [[Vampire Culling]] the names were changed to honor great [[Vampire]] warriors who fell. After the [[Vampire Culling]] ended and the additional 2 walls were created those were also named after great warriors one of which were actually not a [[Vampire]] and was instead a [[Nox]] named [Drazhul].

In-between Wall [Velkhar's Pride] and Wall [Drazhul] is 1 city, 2 minor cities and a few smaller settlements. This area exists as a place for trade between [[Allurea]] and the other nations on [[Lustria]]. Wall [Drazhul] is the only wall allowed to be bypassed by non [[Allurea|Allurean]] citizens with proper documentation for the purposes of trade or diplomacy.

Green Wall: [Velkhar's Pride]
Blue Wall: [Virel's Honor]
Red Wall: [Seraphiel]
Yellow Wall: [Drazhul]
