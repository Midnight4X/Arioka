[Lore:]

- [[Harpy|Harpies]] were considered [[Monsterkin]] for much of history but because of their influence over world politics due their control over the [[Sky Islands]] and war against the [[Demon Royals]] it was agreed that [[Harpy|Harpies]] be removed from the [[Monsterkin]] species list. They also were not added to the [[Demihuman]] subspecies list again due to their influence. This ensures that they aren't restricted from accessing nations that would have otherwise banned all [[Demihuman|Demihumans]] or all [[Monsterkin]] or both.

- [[Harpy|Harpies]] usually from a young-ish age travel down to the [[Realm of Races]] to be trained in magic as well as other important trades to help the war effort over the [[Sky Islands]]. This is done because [[Harpy]] instructors have their hands too full and can only train other [[Harpy|Harpies]] in melee combat and sky combat. To make up for this they rely on the help of outsiders to train them in magic and some trade professions as they lack the capability to do it themselves.

- [[Harpy|Harpies]] mostly occupy the [Sky Islands]] however there are [[Harpy|Harpies]] that also live in villages or in nests of some forests in the [[Realm of Races]]. The [[Harpy|Harpies]] living in villages are not very aggressive and mostly live in peace. They are uninvolved with the war over the [[Sky Islands]] and have mostly no military training. The ones who live in the forest are aggressive and act much closer to how [[Harpy|Harpies]] were when they were classed as [[Monsterkin]]. They can be reasoned with and befriended but it is harder to do so.

- [[Harpy|Harpies]] have hollow bones like birds but have a complex structural honeycombing inside that make them just as strong as non hollow dense bones while still being lighter leading to greater overall speed.

-  [[Harpy|Harpies]] and [[Vampire|Vampires]] hate each other deeply due to years of fighting against each other. They only stopped fighting due to reaching an agreement to halt fighting so the [[Harpy|Harpies]] could focus on the war against the [[Demon Royals]]. However that resentment is still there and when [[Vampire|Vampires]] and [[Harpy|Harpies]] meet it tends to lead to judgmental looks, arguments and fights.
